About the founder

Founder photo

Making reading feel welcoming for everyone.

Readify was created around a simple idea: a reading platform should feel calm, friendly and easy to use whether someone is discovering their first chapter or returning to a favourite classic.

The platform combines a personalised bookshelf, safe age-aware discovery, reading progress, points and story conversations in one place.
